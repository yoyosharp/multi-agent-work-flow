# inbox — async messages between participants

One line per message, newest last. Sender + recipient are claim labels /
role names (plane, worker-N). No instructions in messages — inform the
situation; the receiver decides and acts per its own rules. Substantive
replies/decisions mirror into worklog/qa.md. The recipient reads addressed
rows on its next scheduled poll or safe checkpoint; no live message channel
is assumed. Append an acknowledgement when consumed; never delete a row.
